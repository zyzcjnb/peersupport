### Support Categories

The following questions use five types of support:

- **Instrumental support:** Practical help or services that assist someone in dealing with a problem (e.g., helping preserve evidence or accompanying someone to contact the university or the police).
- **Informational support:** Information, knowledge, or advice that helps someone understand a problem or decide how to respond (e.g., explaining scam tactics or suggesting ways to assess a suspicious message).
- **Emotional support:** Expressions of care, empathy, understanding, and reassurance (e.g., listening to someone’s experience and offering comfort).
- **Esteem support:** Expressions of respect, affirmation, and confidence in someone’s abilities or judgement (e.g., acknowledging that someone did the right thing by seeking help).
- **Network support:** Support that strengthens a person’s sense of belonging and connection with others (e.g., opportunities to connect with people who have similar experiences or concerns).
