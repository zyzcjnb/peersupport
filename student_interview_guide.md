# Student Interview Guide

## Introduction

Thank you for taking part in this interview. We are interested in your
experiences of encountering campus anti-scam information, learning about
the anti-scam ambassadors, and dealing with scam-related concerns. There
are no right or wrong answers. Please answer based on your own
experiences and views.

## 1. Awareness of and Contact with the Ambassadors

1.  How did you first hear about the campus anti-scam ambassadors? What
    do you remember about that?

2.  At the time, what did you understand the ambassadors' role to be?
    What led you to that understanding?

3.  Since then, how, if at all, have you encountered the ambassadors or
    information shared by them? Could you describe one occasion?

4.  What, if anything, did you do after encountering that information or
    activity? What informed your response?

5.  Have you ever spoken directly with an ambassador? If so, could you
    describe one conversation? How did it begin, what did you discuss,
    and what happened afterwards?

## 2. Experiences with Scam-Related Concerns and Help-Seeking

6.  Have you ever received a suspicious message or encountered a
    situation that you were unsure might involve a scam? If so, please
    describe one occasion.

If you have not encountered such a situation, you may proceed to Section
3.

7.  How did you respond to the situation? What factors, if any, shaped
    how you handled it?

8.  Did you discuss the situation with anyone or seek advice? If so,
    whom did you speak to, and what led you to approach that person? If
    not, what options, if any, did you consider?

9.  At the time, did contacting an anti-scam ambassador occur to you? If
    so, what did you consider, and what did you decide? If not, what
    options did you consider for dealing with the situation?

### If you have sought help from an anti-scam ambassador

10. Please describe one occasion when you contacted an ambassador about
    a scam-related concern. What was happening at the time? What led you
    to contact an ambassador?

11. Before contacting the ambassador, what were you hoping to get from
    the conversation?

12. How did the ambassador respond? What did they say or do?

13. How did that response relate to what you needed or expected at the
    time? What, if anything, did you find useful? Were there any aspects
    of your concern that remained unresolved?

### If you have not sought help from an anti-scam ambassador

14. How, if at all, did you deal with the situation afterwards? What
    factors shaped what you did or chose not to do?

## 3. Views on Support and Help-Seeking

15. Based on your encounters or what you know about the programme, how
    do you understand the role of anti-scam ambassadors on campus? What
    has shaped that view?

16. If you had a scam-related concern and wanted to check information or
    seek assistance, how would you decide whom to approach? What would
    you take into account, and why?

17. If you were considering whether to tell someone about a scam-related
    experience, what would you take into account? How might those
    considerations affect whether you spoke to someone or whom you
    approached?

18. Based on your experience or what you know about the programme, in
    what circumstances, if any, might you contact an anti-scam
    ambassador? In what circumstances might you seek help elsewhere?
    What would inform those choices?

## 4. Reflections and Suggestions

19. Overall, what are your views of the anti-scam ambassador programme?
    Based on your own experience or what you have observed, what
    effects, if any, have you noticed on yourself or other students?

20. What changes, if any, would you suggest for the programme in the
    future? What makes you suggest these changes?

21. Is there anything else about your experiences with anti-scam
    information, the ambassadors, or scam-related concerns that you
    would like to add?
