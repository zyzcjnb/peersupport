# Understanding Peer Support for Scam Prevention on Campus

This repository contains the research materials for a study examining how university-organized anti-scam ambassador programmes operate and how students experience peer support in campus scam prevention.

The repository includes the following four files:

1. **Student Survey** – Examines students’ awareness of anti-scam ambassadors, everyday interactions, scam experiences, help-seeking behaviour, and perceptions of peer support.

2. **Anti-Scam Ambassador Survey** – Examines ambassadors’ roles, training, prevention activities, support practices, challenges, and support needs.

3. **Student Interview Guide** – Provides semi-structured interview questions exploring students’ understanding of the ambassador role, experiences with peer support, help-seeking decisions, and suggestions for improvement.

4. **Anti-Scam Ambassador Interview Guide** – Provides semi-structured interview questions exploring ambassadors’ preparation, outreach activities, individual support experiences, and challenges in providing assistance.

All materials are provided for academic research purposes.
